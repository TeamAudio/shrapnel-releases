# Support

For Shrapnel product information, documentation, and downloads, visit:

https://techaudio.io/projects/shrapnel

## Installation Help

If you need help installing Shrapnel, first check the Shrapnel manual:

https://techaudio.io/projects/shrapnel/manuals/1.0.0

When contacting support, please include:

- Your operating system and version.
- Your DAW and version (or "Standalone").
- The Shrapnel version you installed.
- The plugin format you are using: Standalone, VST3, AU, or CLAP.
- A short description of what happened and what you expected to happen.

## License And Activation Help

For license, purchase, or activation issues, contact Tech Audio support through
the support channel listed on the Tech Audio website:

https://techaudio.io/contact

Please do not post license keys, activation codes, order numbers, or private
account details in public GitHub issues or discussions.
